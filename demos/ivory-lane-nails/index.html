Why it lasts

Prep is 80% of a good set

Most chips come from rushed prep, not bad polish. We take the extra ten minutes so your nails make it to the next appointment.

  • Dry cuticle work with sterilised, autoclaved tools
  • Thin layers, each fully LED-cured — no lifting at the edges
  • Professional-only gel brands, HEMA-free options on request
  • Chip in the first week? Come back, we fix it free

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a deposit?

Only for acrylic full sets and appointments over 90 minutes — R100, taken off your total on the day.

What if I'm running late?

Message us. Up to 10 minutes we can usually still do the full service; later than that we may need to shorten it or move you.

How do I cancel or move a booking?

Reply to your confirmation message at least 24 hours before. Late cancellations on deposit bookings forfeit the deposit.

Gel, BIAB or acrylic — which one?

Gel for colour on healthy nails, BIAB to strengthen and grow weak nails, acrylic for length and drama. Not sure? Book a gel overlay slot and we'll advise at the chair.

Can I bring a design photo?

Please do. Add it to your WhatsApp confirmation and we'll quote any nail-art extras before we start.
