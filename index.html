You reply with what you want different. I change it. We do that twice, then the deposit is due and the rest of the pages get built.

Domain pointed, SSL on, analytics and search console connected, site submitted to Google. From there the care plan keeps it patched and backed up.

Questions

The things people ask first

How is this so much cheaper than the agency quote?

An agency quote carries a project manager, a designer, a developer and an office. This is one person who does all three jobs and works from home. The code is the same standard — there is just less of a business attached to it.

Are the two examples real clients?

No, and I will not pretend otherwise. Crescendo Music Academy and Cape Meridian Tours are demonstration builds for invented companies, made so you can judge the work before hiring me. Everything on them is real and working — the code, the speed, the forms — only the businesses are made up.

Do I own the site?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and the site's files are yours. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, I hand over everything — no hostage-taking.

I am not in South Africa. Does that work?

It does. Most of the work is asynchronous and happens over email, so a time difference rarely matters. When a call is needed I will make your working hours work — Teams or Google Meet, whichever you already use. Prices are set in rand and converted to your currency at the day's rate — Wise, PayPal or card.

What is the monthly fee actually for?

Hosting and the SSL certificate, weekly backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and up to two small content changes a month — new prices, new photos, changed hours. Skip it if you would rather host it yourself; the once-off build price does not change.

How long does it take?

Starter is usually live within a week of the deposit. Standard takes about two weeks, mostly waiting on photos and copy from you. Store and custom builds are quoted with a timeline.

I do not have photos or written content.

Most people do not. I write the first draft of the copy from your answers and you correct it. For photos, phone photos taken in good light are usually fine, and I will tell you exactly what to shoot.

Can you fix or take over the site I already have?

Often, yes. Send me the address and I will tell you honestly whether it is worth repairing or worth rebuilding.
