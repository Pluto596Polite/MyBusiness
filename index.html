06 / Questions

The things people ask first

How can quality work cost this little?

No office, no sales team and no layers of management in the price. Proven building blocks are reused and refined from project to project, so time goes into what is unique about your business — not into reinventing the basics. The standard of the code doesn't drop; the overhead does.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Then it isn't finished. Two rounds of changes are included, and the work is checked against your brief before handover. You see a free preview before paying anything, and if it isn't what you wanted at that stage, you walk away owing nothing.

Static, dynamic, web app or phone app — which do I need?

Most small businesses need a static site: it's fastest, cheapest and has the least to go wrong. Go dynamic if you take bookings or want to edit content yourself. A web app is for logins, orders or dashboards. A phone app only makes sense if customers will open it often — I'll tell you honestly if a website does the job.

Why are some running costs "TBD in quote"?

Servers, databases and app backends are priced by how much they're used. A booking site for one salon costs very differently from an ordering app for three branches. I work it out once I know what you need and put it in writing before you commit — no surprises later.

Are the examples real clients?

No, and I won't pretend otherwise. Crescendo Music Academy, Cape Meridian Tours and Ivory Lane Nail Bar are demonstration builds for invented companies, made so you can judge the work before hiring me. Everything on them is real and working — only the businesses are made up.

What happens after the site is live?

You're looked after. The care plan covers updates, backups and small changes, and questions get answered. Everything is also documented and handed over — code, logins in your name, and notes on how it fits together — so you are never stuck or dependent on anyone to keep your site alive.

Do I own the site or app?

Yes. The domain and any store accounts are registered in your name and the code is yours. If you ever want to move, I hand over everything — no hostage-taking.

I'm not in South Africa. Does that work?

It does. Most work is asynchronous over email, so time differences rarely matter. Calls on Teams or Google Meet in your working hours. Prices are set in rand and converted at the day's rate — Wise, PayPal or card.

What is the monthly fee actually for?

R400 a month covers hosting and the SSL certificate, weekly backups, security updates, uptime monitoring, and up to two small content changes a month — new prices, new photos, changed hours. Skip it and host yourself if you prefer; the build price doesn't change.

How long does it take?

One-page static sites are usually live within a week of the deposit. Dynamic sites take two to three weeks, mostly waiting on photos and copy. Web and phone apps are quoted with a timeline and delivered in stages.

I don't have photos or written content.

Most people don't. I write the first draft of the copy from your answers and you correct it. Phone photos taken in good light are usually fine — I'll tell you exactly what to shoot.

Is TeamViewer remote help safe?

Yes. A session only starts when you open TeamViewer and give me your one-time code, and you see everything I do. Close the window and it ends. I never ask for your passwords.
